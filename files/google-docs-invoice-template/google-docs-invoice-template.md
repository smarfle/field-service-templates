# How to use this template
1. Make a private copy before entering customer details. Replace the bracketed fields and add your logo or delete its placeholder.  
2. Fill in the invoice number, dates, bill-to details and service address. Give each invoice a unique number.  
3. Type each description, quantity, rate and amount. This is a document, not a calculator. Multiply quantity by rate, then add the line amounts for the subtotal.  
4. Subtract the agreed discount, calculate any applicable tax, then add tax for the total. Subtract amount paid for the balance due. Confirm tax treatment with your adviser.  
5. The sample arithmetic is $295 minus $25 plus $18.90 tax, giving $288.90 total. After $100 paid, $188.90 remains. Do not copy the example tax rate without checking your own requirements.  
6. Duplicate the document for each invoice. Delete unused item rows and keep the invoice page together. The sample and these notes are not part of the customer invoice.  
7. Print only the invoice page or export just that page to PDF from the print dialog. The top PDF and Word links download the whole template, including the sample and notes. Check payment instructions before sending.
