# How to use this template
1. Make a private copy. Fill the business and customer blocks, service address, unique quote number, issue date and valid-until date.  
2. Describe the exact work and quantities before entering prices. Include the assumptions and exclusions that define what the fixed total covers.  
3. Enter each quantity, rate and line amount manually. Add the line amounts for the subtotal and calculate any applicable tax. This document does not calculate totals automatically.  
4. Confirm that the fixed total covers the promised scope. The sample is $250 + $45 + $0 tax \= $295. Its zero tax rate is fictional, not tax advice. Do not copy that assumption without checking your work and location.  
5. State the proposed schedule, deposit if any, balance due date, payment method, warranty or correction process and cancellation terms. Have local counsel review contract requirements and attach required notices.  
6. Have the customer review and sign the completed version. Confirm the start date separately. Obtain written approval of later scope or price changes rather than adding unapproved charges.  
7. Delete unused rows, the Sample and usage notes from the customer version. The Word and PDF links download the full template. Print only the quote page if needed, check page fit after editing and keep the accepted version privately.
