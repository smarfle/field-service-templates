# How to use this template
1. Make a private copy and enter the business details. Choose the standard receipt page or the two-receipts-per-page option, then remove the layout you do not need.  
2. Give each payment a receipt number and record the date money was actually received. Enter the payer name and a clear service, invoice or job reference.  
3. Enter the amount received on this payment, not the whole invoice total unless it was paid in full. Calculate the balance remaining after all payments. The document does not calculate money fields automatically.  
4. Mark the payment method with [x]. For a check, record its number. For a card or bank transfer, a short transaction reference is enough. Do not record full card numbers, security codes, bank credentials or Social Security numbers.  
5. The sample invoice total is $288.90. An earlier $100 payment plus the $188.90 received today clears the balance. The new receipt is for $188.90 only. A deposit or partial payment should be described as such.  
6. The receiver signs and gives the customer a copy. If two printed receipts document the same payment, use the same number and label one Customer copy and the other Business copy. Do not count them as two payments.  
7. Delete the Sample and notes before sharing a completed receipt. The Word and PDF links download the whole template. Print only the chosen receipt page, check the preview and keep a private record with the related invoice. Never issue a receipt for money not yet received.
