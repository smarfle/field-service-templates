Excluded services: equipment replacement, refrigerant, major repairs, after-hours calls and unrelated equipment. These need separate approval and pricing.

# How to use this template
1. Make a private copy. Identify both legal parties, the service locations, covered equipment and the main agreement. Do not leave unclear whether the SLA adds work or only sets service levels.  
2. Choose targets the crew can meet. Distinguish a human response from arrival and completion. State business hours, time zone, holidays and how requests outside hours are timed.  
3. Complete the priority table, request channel and escalation contact. Use examples that match the business. Do not promise emergency or round-the-clock coverage if it is not staffed.  
4. Define dependencies that can pause a clock, the evidence recorded and how often the customer gets updates. Record request, response and completion times so performance can be checked.  
5. Choose a specific remedy or write none where lawful and agreed. Define its calculation, cap, claim period and approval process. In the sample, one eligible miss earns $15, with a $60 monthly cap; the figures are examples.  
6. Have a local attorney review credits, consumer rights, exclusions, term, termination and conflicts with the main contract. This general form does not replace required notices or a trade-specific agreement.  
7. Fill all fields, delete the Sample and notes, and have both parties sign the same dated version. The download links export the whole template. Keep signed copies and service reports privately and review targets when scope changes.
