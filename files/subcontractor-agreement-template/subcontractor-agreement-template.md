# Scope schedule and coordination
Subcontractor will perform the agreed scope with reasonable skill and care using qualified personnel. It controls its lawful means and methods while coordinating agreed milestones and site safety. Contractor will supply agreed access, information and predecessor work. Report delays or concealed conditions to [contact] promptly. Adjustments in time and cost require written agreement. No entire prime contract is incorporated by reference unless the identified provisions were supplied and expressly accepted.

# Changes

For an immediate hazard, stop affected work and notify [contact]; emergency protective work is limited to [agreed procedure and spending limit].

# How to use this template
1. Make a private copy. Identify the legal parties, owner, site, notice contacts and the exact documents that form the agreement.  
2. Have a local construction attorney review payment, retainage, lien notices and releases, indemnity, insurance and termination before signing. State rules can override a contract term.  
3. Write a measurable scope and exclusions. List who supplies materials and permits, the schedule dependencies and how change orders are approved.  
4. Set a clear invoice due date and retainage release trigger. Do not leave payment dependent on an undefined event or insert an unconditional waiver before funds clear.  
5. Verify licenses and insurance with the relevant issuer or insurer. Match indemnity and endorsement terms to the actual project and available coverage.  
6. Complete warranty, suspension, termination and dispute fields. Delete unused choices, the Sample and these template notes. Both parties sign and keep the same final version.  
7. Save approved changes, invoices, payment proof, releases and closeout records privately. Do not treat this document as evidence that a required permit, license or payment already exists.
