# Free Appointment Spreadsheet

For service businesses that take bookings by phone and text and need a simple appointment book that shows the day, the week and any clash.

**[Open the template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1nJWGQNYYIjJkIV_s9N9lL5jNVESWMElTbo81AJ0Wa2c/edit)** &middot; **[Make a copy](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1nJWGQNYYIjJkIV_s9N9lL5jNVESWMElTbo81AJ0Wa2c/copy)** &middot; Google Sheet, free, no sign-up

## What is inside

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | Totals and views built from Appointments, no typing needed. |
| Appointments | Columns Appointment ID, Date, Start time, Duration (minutes), End time, Customer name, Phone, Email, Service and 8 more. |
| Day view | Totals and views built from Appointments, no typing needed. |
| Week view | Totals and views built from Appointments, no typing needed. |
| Settings | Your own dropdown lists (service, default minutes, weekday number, weekday and open), so the choices match how you work. |

## How to fill it in

1. Set opening hours, services with default minutes and staff on Settings.
2. Add each booking on Appointments with date, start time, service and staff member.
3. Check Day view and Week view before you confirm a new time.
4. Tick Confirmation sent once the customer has the details.

## A tip from the field

Staff overlap and Customer overlap flag two bookings in one slot, and the business hours alert catches times outside your hours.
