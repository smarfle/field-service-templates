# Free Balance Sheet Template

For an owner getting ready for a loan, a lease or a meeting with the accountant, who needs assets, liabilities and equity on one page.

**[Open the template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1D-qw7RrKHzdAUKYMZaR8wbIxb85B0zCrN32dbbhenZ8/edit)** &middot; **[Make a copy](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1D-qw7RrKHzdAUKYMZaR8wbIxb85B0zCrN32dbbhenZ8/copy)** &middot; Google Sheet, free, no sign-up

## What is inside

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Balance Sheet | Totals and views built from Accounts and Balances, no typing needed. |
| Accounts | Columns Account ID, Account name, Classification and Quick asset. |
| Balances | Columns Account ID, Balance date, Balance (USD) and Notes. |
| Settings | Your own dropdown lists (current asset), so the choices match how you work. |

## How to fill it in

1. List your accounts on Accounts with their classification.
2. Enter each account's balance on a given date on Balances.
3. Pick the date on Settings, then read Balance Sheet.
4. Compare two dates to see how cash, debt and equity moved.

## A tip from the field

Take balances from statements dated the same day, bank, loan and card, so the page lines up.
