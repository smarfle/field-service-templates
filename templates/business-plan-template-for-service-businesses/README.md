# Free Business Plan Template for Service Businesses

For someone starting or growing a home service business who needs a plan for a bank, a partner or simply their own next twelve months.

**[Open the template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Y2QSdzYXgr5uyr-CGckM5NyjxvY9GDWCgQaiVG81hrY/edit)** &middot; **[Make a copy](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Y2QSdzYXgr5uyr-CGckM5NyjxvY9GDWCgQaiVG81hrY/copy)** &middot; Google Doc, free, no sign-up

## What is inside

- Executive summary
- Company and owner
- Services and pricing
- Local market and competitors
- Getting customers and delivering work
- Marketing and getting customers
- Operations
- Team and responsibilities
- Startup costs and funding
- First year forecast and milestones
- Break even
- Goals and milestones
- Summary and company
- Services and market
- Marketing and operations
- Startup funding and risks

A filled sample shows how a finished copy reads.

## How to fill it in

1. Write the summary last, after the other sections.
2. Describe services, pricing, the local market and competitors.
3. Explain how you will win customers and run daily operations.
4. Fill in startup costs, a first year forecast and the break even point.

## A tip from the field

Build the forecast from jobs per week times average ticket. That is easier to check than a revenue figure picked from the air.
