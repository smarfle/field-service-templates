# Free Change Order Template

For jobs where the scope changes after work starts, and both sides need the new price and finish date in writing before the extra work begins.

**[Open the template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1pLAUydoXxsoQtSNN5c11I0FnqiIHQCuV9gcNG2IDn6o/edit)** &middot; **[Make a copy](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1pLAUydoXxsoQtSNN5c11I0FnqiIHQCuV9gcNG2IDn6o/copy)** &middot; Google Doc, free, no sign-up

## What is inside

- Project and customer, Contractor
- Original contract reference, Change #, Change date
- Change description
- Reason
- Cost change in USD, Work or pricing detail, Signed amount
- Contract amount in USD
- Schedule change
- Schedule conditions
- Payment terms for this change
- Customer approval, Contractor approval

A filled sample shows how a finished copy reads.

## How to fill it in

1. Reference the original estimate or contract.
2. Describe the change and the reason for it.
3. Show the price change, the new total and the new completion date.
4. Get the customer's signature before doing the extra work.

## A tip from the field

Number change orders in sequence. The final invoice is then the original price plus each signed change.
