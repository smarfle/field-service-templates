# Free Employee Handbook Template

For a small service business hiring its first field crew, which needs written rules on hours, pay, safety, vehicles and conduct in customers' homes.

**[Open the template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1PIVjALOwpBxpD3u6JsM6wra-h5HwO1d5TmljB9Ucbi8/edit)** &middot; **[Make a copy](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1PIVjALOwpBxpD3u6JsM6wra-h5HwO1d5TmljB9Ucbi8/copy)** &middot; Google Doc, free, no sign-up

## What is inside

- Company details
- Welcome
- Employment basics
- Hours and attendance
- Pay and working time
- Pay and timekeeping
- Overtime
- Breaks and meals
- Time off and holidays
- Safety and field work
- Safety and reporting
- Vehicles and equipment
- Uniforms and customer homes
- Phones and social media
- Concerns and employment changes
- Harassment and complaints

A filled sample shows how a finished copy reads.

## How to fill it in

1. Fill in your company details and the welcome.
2. Edit hours, pay, overtime, breaks and time off to match your state's rules.
3. Adjust the safety, vehicle, uniform and phone rules for your trade.
4. Have each employee sign that they received it.

## A tip from the field

Wage and break rules differ by state, so check those sections against your state labor office.
