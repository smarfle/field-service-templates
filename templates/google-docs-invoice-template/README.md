# Free Google Docs Invoice Template

For businesses that prefer a document they can type into, print or send as a PDF, with bill-to details, a service address and a worked sample.

**[Open the template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1wkwFePBRwPe6hqGEDbsoUL9k43StUT32Guh6kfnUNoM/edit)** &middot; **[Make a copy](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1wkwFePBRwPe6hqGEDbsoUL9k43StUT32Guh6kfnUNoM/copy)** &middot; Google Doc, free, no sign-up

## What is inside

- From, Bill to
- Invoice #, Issue date, Due date
- Service address
- Description, Qty, Rate, Amount
- Totals in USD
- Payment methods and terms
- Notes

A filled sample shows how a finished copy reads.

## How to fill it in

1. Make your own copy and add your logo and business details.
2. Fill in the invoice number, dates, customer and service address.
3. Type each line with quantity, rate and amount, then subtotal, discount, tax and balance.
4. Print or save only the invoice page as a PDF.

## A tip from the field

A document does not add numbers for you. If you want totals worked out, use the Google Sheets invoice template instead.
