# Free Google Docs Receipt Template

For payments taken in person, cash, check or card, where the customer wants proof right away. Two receipts fit on one page.

**[Open the template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1YP66C5SKoIAVW4ia_0i-qyIjf8twuSw9QrQRfqocf0A/edit)** &middot; **[Make a copy](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1YP66C5SKoIAVW4ia_0i-qyIjf8twuSw9QrQRfqocf0A/copy)** &middot; Google Doc, free, no sign-up

## What is inside

- Business, Receipt details
- Received from
- Payment for
- Amount received in USD, Balance remaining in USD
- Payment reference
- Notes
- Received by and signature
- Business

A filled sample shows how a finished copy reads.

## How to fill it in

1. Make a copy and add your business details.
2. Fill in the date, the customer, what was paid for and the amount.
3. Note the payment method and any balance left.
4. Give one copy to the customer and keep the other.

## A tip from the field

Number receipts in order, so a missing number is easy to notice.
