# Free Google Sheets Dispatch Schedule

For an office that assigns several technicians a day and needs one screen showing who is booked, who is free and which jobs nobody has yet.

**[Open the template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1RSlc02UHiYpvRIXF_essJA3VgqHVMo82Gtg1IxNUoaA/edit)** &middot; **[Make a copy](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1RSlc02UHiYpvRIXF_essJA3VgqHVMo82Gtg1IxNUoaA/copy)** &middot; Google Sheet, free, no sign-up

## What is inside

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | Totals and views built from Jobs and Technicians, no typing needed. |
| Jobs | Columns Job ID, Date, Window start, Window end, Client name, Phone, Street, City, ZIP and 7 more. |
| Technicians | Columns Technician ID, Name, Phone, Skills or trade, Work start, Work end, Max hours per day and Active. |
| Day Board | Totals and views built from Jobs and Technicians, no typing needed. |
| Week | Totals and views built from Jobs and Technicians, no typing needed. |
| Settings | Your own dropdown lists (service, priority, status and active), so the choices match how you work. |

## How to fill it in

1. Add technicians with skills, work hours and max hours per day.
2. Enter jobs with a time window, address, estimated hours and priority.
3. Assign a technician and a stop order, then check the Day Board for capacity and conflicts.
4. Send the route with the Google Maps link on each job.

## A tip from the field

The Window overlap and Capacity alert columns catch double booking before the technician discovers it at the door.
