# Free Google Sheets Expense Tracker

For owners who pay for materials, fuel and tools with a mix of cards and cash and want every receipt tied to a client or a job long before tax time.

**[Open the template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1WfW-AUKl9-JoTG6qRtfdZwRyaHlB7LZrUNiH3OnJ3HI/edit)** &middot; **[Make a copy](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1WfW-AUKl9-JoTG6qRtfdZwRyaHlB7LZrUNiH3OnJ3HI/copy)** &middot; Google Sheet, free, no sign-up

## What is inside

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | Totals and views built from Expenses, Clients and Jobs, no typing needed. |
| Expenses | Columns Date, Vendor, Amount, Category, Description, Payment method, Client ID, Job ID, Tax paid and 5 more. |
| Clients | Columns Client ID and Client name. |
| Jobs | Columns Job ID, Job name and Client ID. |
| Settings | Your own dropdown lists (categories, payment methods and employees), so the choices match how you work. |

## How to fill it in

1. Type each purchase on Expenses with vendor, amount, category and payment method.
2. Paste a receipt link, a photo saved in Drive is enough, so nothing gets lost.
3. Add the Client ID and Job ID when a cost belongs to one job.
4. Mark reimbursable items and who paid, so you know what you owe your team.

## A tip from the field

The Duplicate check column catches the same receipt typed twice, which happens easily at month end.
