# Free Google Sheets Mileage Log

For anyone who drives between jobs and wants a clean mileage record for reimbursement or tax time, with odometer checks.

**[Open the template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/10AsCSAvHQGBCFiySR0GsAjbbraYb_JttNdnPzmtCB08/edit)** &middot; **[Make a copy](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/10AsCSAvHQGBCFiySR0GsAjbbraYb_JttNdnPzmtCB08/copy)** &middot; Google Sheet, free, no sign-up

## What is inside

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | Totals and views built from Trips, no typing needed. |
| Monthly Log | Totals and views built from Trips, no typing needed. |
| Trips | Columns Trip ID, Date, Driver, Vehicle, Start location, End location, Purpose, Customer or job, Start odometer and 7 more. |
| Settings | Your own dropdown lists (drivers, vehicles and business or personal), so the choices match how you work. |

## How to fill it in

1. Add drivers and vehicles on Settings.
2. Log each trip on Trips with start and end location, purpose and odometer readings.
3. Mark business or personal and round trips.
4. Read Monthly Log and Dashboard for business miles and reimbursement owed.

## A tip from the field

Odometer gaps get flagged, so a trip missing between two readings shows up while you still remember it.
