# Free Google Sheets Payment Tracker

For businesses that take card, bank, cash and check payments and need to match what customers paid against what actually reached the bank.

**[Open the template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1RUmq96JDN5nSM6NaETqRBaEZah-iJWegRodmISyunno/edit)** &middot; **[Make a copy](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1RUmq96JDN5nSM6NaETqRBaEZah-iJWegRodmISyunno/copy)** &middot; Google Sheet, free, no sign-up

## What is inside

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | Totals and views built from Payments, Invoices and Deposits, no typing needed. |
| Payments | Columns Payment ID, Date received, Customer, Invoice number, Type, Amount (positive), Method, Reference, Processing fee and 6 more. |
| Invoices | Columns Invoice number, Customer, Invoice date, Invoice total, Paid to date, Balance, Status and Notes. |
| Deposits | Columns Deposit batch ID, Deposit date, Bank statement amount, Expected total, Difference (bank minus expected), Matched and Notes. |
| Settings | Your own dropdown lists (payment method and type), so the choices match how you work. |

## How to fill it in

1. Record each payment with the invoice it pays, the method, a reference and any processing fee.
2. Group deposited payments into a deposit batch with the bank date.
3. Compare Expected total with the bank statement amount on Deposits.
4. Use Invoices to spot overpayments and balances still open.

## A tip from the field

Cash and checks that were never deposited show in the deposit alert, the usual reason the bank comes up short.
