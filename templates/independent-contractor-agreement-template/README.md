# Free Independent Contractor Agreement Template

For owners who bring in a 1099 helper or a specialist and want the work, pay, tools, insurance and the relationship written down.

**[Open the template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1P70O_Uc6D7v1kIDl3IGehDY4lzCZ3g2jUrT4LTrp2-U/edit)** &middot; **[Make a copy](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1P70O_Uc6D7v1kIDl3IGehDY4lzCZ3g2jUrT4LTrp2-U/copy)** &middot; Google Doc, free, no sign-up

## What is inside

- Parties and engagement
- Services and schedule
- Payment and invoicing
- Working relationship and responsibilities
- Independent contractor status
- Tools licenses and insurance
- Safety and property
- Information work product and ending the agreement
- Confidentiality
- Ownership of work
- Term and termination
- Disputes and general terms
- Official guidance to check

A filled sample shows how a finished copy reads.

## How to fill it in

1. Fill in both parties and the engagement.
2. Describe the services, the schedule and how invoices get paid.
3. Set who supplies tools, licenses and insurance.
4. Cover confidentiality, ownership of work and how either side can end it.

## A tip from the field

A contract does not settle worker status by itself. How the work happens in practice matters, so check with your accountant.
