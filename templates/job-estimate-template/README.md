# Free Job Estimate Template

For bigger jobs where the price rests on assumptions, so the estimate spells out scope, line items, deposit and what could change the number.

**[Open the template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1X8t2CE6X-gqBkVNzM99NhQx48nv_PM2fuSxG-1fzGuI/edit)** &middot; **[Make a copy](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1X8t2CE6X-gqBkVNzM99NhQx48nv_PM2fuSxG-1fzGuI/copy)** &middot; Google Doc, free, no sign-up

## What is inside

- Business, Customer and job address
- Estimate #, Date, Valid until
- Scope summary
- Description, Qty, Unit, Rate, Amount
- Estimate totals in USD
- Assumptions
- Exclusions
- Terms and payment
- Customer acceptance, Date

A filled sample shows how a finished copy reads.

## How to fill it in

1. Fill in the customer, the job address and a short description of the work.
2. Add line items with quantities and prices, then tax and deposit.
3. Write down the assumptions and exclusions the price depends on.
4. Send it and keep the signed copy with the job.

## A tip from the field

Written assumptions turn a surprise on site into a simple change order instead of an argument.
