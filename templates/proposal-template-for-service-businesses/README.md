# Free Proposal Template for Service Businesses

For bigger or competitive jobs, commercial cleaning, remodels or multi-visit plans, where a one-page quote is too thin and the customer compares several bids.

**[Open the template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1XjcTHaKnwNdcIhIyGUFzr4ZxgYS85TaM7RLYNIUrC6I/edit)** &middot; **[Make a copy](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1XjcTHaKnwNdcIhIyGUFzr4ZxgYS85TaM7RLYNIUrC6I/copy)** &middot; Google Doc, free, no sign-up

## What is inside

- Prepared for
- Prepared by
- Project
- Your main objective
- Scope and delivery
- About us
- The customer needs
- Proposed solution and scope
- Timeline
- Included and excluded
- Price and acceptance
- Pricing options
- Warranty and terms
- Next steps and acceptance

A filled sample shows how a finished copy reads.

## How to fill it in

1. Restate the customer's needs and your main objective in their words.
2. Lay out the scope, the timeline and what is included and excluded.
3. Offer two or three pricing options.
4. Close with warranty, terms and the acceptance step.

## A tip from the field

Pricing options change the customer's question from whether to hire you to which option to pick.
