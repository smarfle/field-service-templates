# Free Service Contract Template

For recurring property services, lawn, snow and cleaning, where both sides should agree on schedule, access, price and cancellation before the first visit.

**[Open the template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1rKvh5ejaZ8cV_xnOhWHBrcyHN0bZAUFz7Q5v_iUYHvE/edit)** &middot; **[Make a copy](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1rKvh5ejaZ8cV_xnOhWHBrcyHN0bZAUFz7Q5v_iUYHvE/copy)** &middot; Google Doc, free, no sign-up

## What is inside

- Parties and service details
- Scope and changes
- Scheduling and access
- Payment service quality and cancellation
- Price and payment
- Service quality and warranty
- Cancellation and renewal
- Responsibility and disputes
- Optional lawn care schedule
- Optional snow removal schedule
- Optional cleaning schedule
- Optional HVAC maintenance schedule
- Official guidance to check

A filled sample shows how a finished copy reads.

## How to fill it in

1. Fill in the parties, the property and the services.
2. Set the schedule and how you get access.
3. Agree on price, payment timing, quality and cancellation.
4. Keep or delete the optional lawn, snow and cleaning schedules, then sign.

## A tip from the field

Have a lawyer in your state read it once before you use it with customers.
