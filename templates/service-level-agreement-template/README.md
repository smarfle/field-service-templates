# Free Service Level Agreement Template

For commercial and property management clients who ask how fast you respond and what happens if you miss it.

**[Open the template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1KzYMTUo56nCjSXz6Ipcbmi1rJTaCyZ1fMWVfRkqywpQ/edit)** &middot; **[Make a copy](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1KzYMTUo56nCjSXz6Ipcbmi1rJTaCyZ1fMWVfRkqywpQ/copy)** &middot; Google Doc, free, no sign-up

## What is inside

- Parties and covered services
- Service hours and requests
- Priority targets
- Service delivery and agreement terms
- Scheduling and access
- Reporting and customer responsibilities
- Exclusions and remedies
- Review and changes
- Term and termination

A filled sample shows how a finished copy reads.

## How to fill it in

1. List the covered services, service hours and how requests reach you.
2. Set response and fix targets for each priority.
3. Agree on reporting, customer responsibilities and exclusions.
4. Agree on the remedy for missed targets and a review date.

## A tip from the field

Only promise targets your slowest week can meet. An agreement you miss every month costs more than it wins.
