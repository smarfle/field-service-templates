# Free Small Business Budget Template

For owners setting a budget for the year who want to compare each month's income and spending with the plan, without building formulas.

**[Open the template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Euic-L8UDL_Kn5YV29M5t7Nq3peHZHH30SJwHYbeqTc/edit)** &middot; **[Make a copy](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Euic-L8UDL_Kn5YV29M5t7Nq3peHZHH30SJwHYbeqTc/copy)** &middot; Google Sheet, free, no sign-up

## What is inside

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Budget vs Actuals | Totals and views built from Budget and Actuals, no typing needed. |
| Annual View | Totals and views built from Budget and Actuals, no typing needed. |
| Budget | Columns Budget ID, Month (any date), Category, Amount and Notes. |
| Actuals | Columns Actual ID, Date, Category, Amount and Description. |
| Settings | Your own dropdown lists (category, group and allowed group), so the choices match how you work. |

## How to fill it in

1. Review categories and groups on Settings.
2. Enter planned amounts by month on Budget.
3. Enter what really happened on Actuals.
4. Read Budget vs Actuals for the month and year to date, and Annual View for the whole year.

## A tip from the field

Budget slow months honestly. A plan that assumes every month looks like June will be wrong by February.
