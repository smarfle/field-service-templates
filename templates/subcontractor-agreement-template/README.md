# Free Subcontractor Agreement Template

For general contractors and trades that hire another company for part of a project and need scope, payment, insurance and closeout agreed up front.

**[Open the template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1oFlJK9alIQ8TNUVb0Zag5JKIfk4GItMMkpKi3AjzP44/edit)** &middot; **[Make a copy](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1oFlJK9alIQ8TNUVb0Zag5JKIfk4GItMMkpKi3AjzP44/copy)** &middot; Google Doc, free, no sign-up

## What is inside

- Parties and project
- Scope schedule and coordination
- Changes
- Payment insurance and risk
- Invoices payment and retainage
- Insurance and indemnity
- Permits safety and property
- Closeout warranty and signatures
- Lien waivers and closeout
- Warranty and correction
- Term suspension and termination
- Disputes and entire agreement

A filled sample shows how a finished copy reads.

## How to fill it in

1. Fill in the parties and the project.
2. Describe the scope, the schedule and how changes get approved.
3. Set invoicing, payment, retainage and insurance terms.
4. Agree on permits, safety, lien waivers, warranty and termination.

## A tip from the field

Collect the certificate of insurance before the sub starts, not when the first invoice arrives.
