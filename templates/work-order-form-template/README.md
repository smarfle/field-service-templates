# Free Work Order Form Template

For technicians who still like paper on the clipboard, a one-page job record with the work requested, work done, parts, hours and the customer's signature.

**[Open the template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/16oNMqx0hycjJyvINRCPd5GBdYE_jt0jCmiXxod5MIPc/edit)** &middot; **[Make a copy](https://docs.google.com/document/d/16oNMqx0hycjJyvINRCPd5GBdYE_jt0jCmiXxod5MIPc/copy)** &middot; Google Doc, free, no sign-up

## What is inside

- Work order #, Date, Business and phone
- Customer, Customer phone
- Service address
- Requested work
- Technician, Arrival window
- Materials or parts used, Qty, Unit
- Labor hours, Start and finish if recorded
- Before work, After work
- Completion notes and follow-up
- Customer name and signature, Date

A filled sample shows how a finished copy reads.

## How to fill it in

1. Fill in the customer, address and requested work before the visit.
2. On site, record the work done, parts used and labor hours.
3. Have the customer sign and leave them a copy.
4. Hand it in for invoicing the same day.

## A tip from the field

Write down what you found as well as what you did. It answers the callback question a month later.
